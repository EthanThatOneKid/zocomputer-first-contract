A controlled pilot for the IWP-to-marked-image workflow.

The proposed engagement evaluates whether an approved source .iwp program and corresponding marked PDF or image can be detected, registered, reviewed, and safely transformed into a relabeled IWP without changing measurement logic or corrupting the source format.

01 · The proposal

A bounded pilot with a concrete answer at the end

The pilot is designed to reduce uncertainty before production integration. It starts with discovery and a controlled real-pair evaluation, keeps the production systems read-only, and produces the evidence needed to decide whether a larger implementation is justified.

~74
nominal planning hours

Discovery, safe IWP processing, rendering, registration, vision evaluation, audit output, and handoff. Milestones are billed as fixed amounts, not by hours worked.

02 · Scope and deliverables

What the pilot delivers

Discovery and boundary

Machine/software interface inventory, source-of-truth map, security and network constraints, and a proposed API/data model.

IWP processing

UTF-16LE/CRLF-safe parsing and writing for the agreed feature subset, canonical millimetre geometry, deterministic SVG/PNG review artifacts, and before/after audit data.

Vision and matching

Structured marker observations, provider integration, affine or projective registration, one-to-one geometric matching, residuals, ambiguity checks, and confidence gates.

Controlled evaluation

One approved real source-IWP/marked-image pair, a proposed mapping report, a reviewable relabeled IWP, and an audit manifest.

Handoff

API contract draft, implementation notes, runbook, limitations, follow-up backlog, and a production-readiness recommendation.

Provider boundary

Google Gemini is for the synthetic proof only. Real client data uses only client-authorized Claude through GovCloud, subject to client approval.

05 · Acceptance and boundaries

How both sides know the pilot is complete

Acceptance criteria

  1. The agreed sample IWP is read and written without changing encoding, line endings, or non-target content.
  2. The agreed feature subset is rendered into a reviewable canonical image.
  3. The approved vision provider returns structured marker observations for the approved image.
  4. The matcher reports one-to-one assignments, transform residuals, and confidence gates.
  5. The rewritten IWP, audit manifest, and human-review report are produced.
  6. The client receives a recommendation for the next production-integration phase.

Approval window

The client names one technical approver. The approver has five business days after delivery to accept the artifact or identify specific material defects against the agreed criteria.

New features, systems, geometries, environments, or production deployment require a change order.

06 · IP and data

Specific deliverables, protected data, reusable background technology

Client-owned work

The client owns the machine-specific and Discus-specific deliverables created and paid for under the SOW, including agreed integration code, schemas, configuration, mappings, runbook, and final evaluation artifacts.

Background technology

Ethan retains pre-existing and reusable generic parsers, geometry/registration algorithms, AI adapters, test harnesses, know-how, and improvements that do not disclose client Confidential Information.

Client data

IWP files, marked drawings, machine configurations, Discus data, customer information, inspection results, and manufacturing records remain client-controlled Confidential Information.

AI-provider rule: Synthetic fixtures may use Google Gemini. Real client data must not use the free Gemini path. Real-data inference may use only client-authorized Claude through GovCloud after the client approves the endpoint, model, region, retention behavior, logging, and contractual controls.

07 · Assumptions and exclusions

What this price depends on

Assumptions

  • The client provides one approved, authorized source-IWP/marked-image pair.
  • The client provides the machine, Discus, and output documentation needed for the read-only evaluation.
  • The client identifies the owners of quote, production, inventory, inspection, and quality records.
  • Human review remains mandatory before a rewritten IWP is used operationally.
  • The pilot remains read-only against production systems.

Excluded unless added

  • Production deployment or 24/7 support
  • Machine-control commands or automated quality disposition
  • Full ERP/MES/QMS implementation
  • Customer-facing portal development
  • Compliance certification work
  • Guaranteed throughput or accuracy
